So.. Why again? briefly:

  • Authentic, Us, Ours
    • Not accounts on services, only keys we own and control
    • (Tom's account is with those who've vouched for him. We control our delegate keys associations.)
    • Bonus: no fake accounts, no bots
    • Facebook likes, Airbnb ratings, LinkedIn connections.. all of these are owned and live at those services and can only be trusted only when served by them.
    • Our network / your network. Your keys, and not locked to any domain, not mine, not ONE-OF-US.NET's, not nerdster.org's... Just like the Internet isn't Google's or Amazon's.
  • Decentralized
    • No hierarchy, no power center
    • Yours (from your Point of View)
  • Open
    • Let tech play nice together (ONE-OF-US.NET, the Nerdster, HabloTengo are not connected - you tell them who you are, they fetch and verify signed statements from a variety of sources)
    • Published statements are not trusted because of where they came from (what service served them)
      They're trusted (and Portable) because they're signed by your own keys.
    • We demonstrated the Nerdster, a delegate service, could use 100s, 1,000s... without giving any the private component of our identity key.
    • Identity layer establishes identity; any service can leverage that however they like (evolution), eg. Trust a person for rentals, block same for news commentary.

Closing

A different paradigm (like the Internet was).

  • We build the network. We let them use it.
  • Get us on the same page everywhere.
  • Break the algorithm monopoly of our metadata silos.
  • If I enjoy my stay at your Airbnb, who's a good host: you or your Airbnb account?
  • Litter the Internet with statements signed by our own keys grounded in our identities rather than pseudonymous and disconnected in siloed accounts.
